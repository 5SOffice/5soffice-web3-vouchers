# Entitlement Catalog v2

## Entitlement types

### Service unit

A countable right such as one meeting-room hour, one day pass or a print bundle.

### Discount or promotion

A conditional price benefit evaluated against campaign and service rules.

### Time-based access

Access to a feature or service for a defined period.

### Quota or credit

A metered quantity consumed by actions, such as AI document actions or meeting summaries.

### Expert service credit

A right to request a human professional review or consultation, subject to scope and scheduling.

## Required catalog fields

- entitlement type ID and version;
- public name and description;
- issuing tenant/brand and campaign;
- unit and quantity;
- validity period;
- location, service and customer eligibility;
- transferability;
- stacking and partial-use rules;
- reservation expiry;
- redemption and cancellation policy;
- financial and tax treatment where applicable;
- optional Web3 mapping;
- status: draft, active, suspended or retired.
